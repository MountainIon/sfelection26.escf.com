How to read this

  • Broad support means at least 8 groups took a position (or endorsed in the race), at least 80% of them agree, and a majority of both the progressive and the moderate groups that weighed in agree too. Leans means 65–79% agreement. Everything else is Split.
  • For candidate races, the percentage is the share of groups that endorsed anyone in that race who picked that candidate. A race where only one camp endorsed (BART D8, for example) can still read as broad, so check the camp counts under each row.
  • The progressive / moderate grouping is a rough sort based on each group's 2026 slate. Groups added from OpenBallot were sorted by how often their picks match the existing progressive and moderate groups. Several groups (SF Democratic Party, SPUR, LWV, Sierra Club, Women's Political Committee) are labeled civic/issue rather than forced into a camp.
  • Click any Yes, No, or candidate cell to open the organization's own page where it states that position. Column headers open each organization's voter guide. Two exceptions: Faith in Action Bay Area publishes its guide only as an image on OpenBallot, and the SF Young Republicans have no endorsement page we could find, so those columns link to OpenBallot and SFEndorsements.
  • Blank cells mean no endorsement, no position, neutral, or not yet released. LWV is neutral on Prop G; LPOV took no position on G, Prop 2, and Prop 37.
